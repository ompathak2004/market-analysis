PM to Inaugurate and Dedicate Five Development Projects Worth Around ₹3,350 Crore in Ladakh on 15 October


Projects Span Airport Infrastructure, Strategic Road Connectivity, Hydropower, Heritage Conservation and Water Security

PM to Inaugurate New Passenger Terminal Building at Leh Airport

PM to Dedicate 298-km Nimmu-Padum-Darcha Road, Providing Alternative Connectivity to Ladakh and Strengthening Strategic Mobility for the Armed Forces

PM to Inaugurate India’s First Petroglyph Conservation Park in Leh for Preservation of Ladakh’s Ancient Archaeological Heritage

Posted On: 14 OCT 2026 3:15PM by PIB Delhi

Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi will inaugurate and dedicate to the nation five major development projects worth around ₹3,350 crore in Ladakh on 15 October 2026 at around 12:30 PM via video conferencing. He will also address the gathering on the occasion. The five projects will strengthen connectivity, improve energy infrastructure, conserve cultural heritage and enhance water security in the region.

Prime Minister will inaugurate the New Passenger Terminal Building at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport, Leh, built at a cost of ₹640 crore. Spread over 23,800 square metres, the modern terminal is designed to handle 35 lakh passengers annually. Incorporating geothermal energy to enhance energy efficiency and reduce its carbon footprint, the facility reflects the importance of sustainable infrastructure in Ladakh’s ecologically sensitive environment. Blending modern passenger amenities with elements of Ladakh’s rich cultural heritage, the terminal will improve passenger convenience and strengthen air connectivity with the rest of the country.
